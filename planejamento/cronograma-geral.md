24-Month Cybersecurity Study Schedule

104-Week Learning Roadmap

Duration: 24 months
Total: 104 weeks
Weekly commitment: 15–17 hours
Structure: 8 periods of 13 weeks
Primary environment: Linux / Termux (Android)
Programming language: Python

---

Use this structure as the default weekly allocation:

The allocation can be adjusted according to the difficulty of the weekly subject.

10. Recurring Weekly Requirements

Every week must include the following:

1. Study the assigned theory.
2. Complete the practical exercises.
3. Perform the assigned laboratory.
4. Record commands, scripts and relevant outputs.
5. Write or update Markdown notes.
6. Complete a technical English activity.
7. Review mistakes and unresolved questions.
8. Record progress in the weekly folder.
9. Commit relevant work to GitHub after checking for sensitive information.

11. Period Assessment Requirements

At the end of each 13-week period:

- Complete a theoretical assessment.
- Complete a practical assessment.
- Submit the period project.
- Review the required competencies.
- Identify knowledge gaps.
- Update the portfolio.
- Record the period result.

A period is not considered complete merely because all weeks have passed. Essential competencies must be demonstrated.

12. Scheduling and Adaptation Policy

This is a sequence-based schedule, not a rigid calendar.

If a topic requires additional study, the student should reinforce it before progressing to dependent subjects.

Missed study hours must not be compensated by skipping exercises or assessments.

The weekly schedule may be adjusted to account for:

- Difficulty.
- Previous assessment results.
- Technical environment limitations.
- Project requirements.
- Changes in professional priorities.

The detailed lesson plans and daily activities belong in the corresponding weekly folders.

Schedule principle: Consistency over speed. Practical evidence over passive consumption. Competence over completion.
